# Al Boabid Family Legacy

Build me an official website for the"آل بوعبيد" family, similar in style and structure to these Saudi family fund websites:

almajedfamily.com

albassam.family

almulhimeb.org

alnaim.sa

rwabet.org.sa

The site represents the family's fund/foundation and should be professional, modern, and trustworthy, with a visual identity that reflects heritage and family belonging.

1. Language and Direction

Primary language: Arabic, with full right to left (RTL) layout.

Ability to add an English version later (language switcher at the top).

A clear, readable Arabic font (such as Cairo, Tajawal, or IBM Plex Sans Arabic).

2. Visual Identity

A family logo (placeholder text "Al Boabid" until a real logo is provided).

A calm, refined color palette (shades of dark blue, olive green, or gold, with light backgrounds).

Clean design with generous white space and large, high quality images.

Fully responsive (mobile, tablet, desktop).

3. Main Navigation

Home

About Us (family background and lineage)

Family Fund/Foundation (goals, bylaws, board of trustees)

Family Tree / Family Members

Family Achievers (notable accomplishments and figures)

Initiatives and Projects

Services

News (Family News / Fund News)

Library (documents, photos, videos, publications)

Contact Us

Contribute / Donate

4. Homepage

A banner/slider with photos from family events.

A short "About Us" section with a "Read more" link.

A quick stats section (number of family members, year founded, number of initiatives, etc.) with animated counters.

Latest news (cards with image, date, title, and "Details" link).

A section highlighting initiatives or services.

A call to action to join the family communication channel (WhatsApp/Telegram).

A quick contact form (name, email, message).

Footer with: a short blurb, quick links, contact details (phone, email, location map), social media icons (Twitter/X, Instagram, Snapchat, TikTok, YouTube), and copyright.

5. About Us Page

Historical background on the family's origin, lineage, and homeland.

Vision, mission, and goals of the fund/foundation (if applicable).

Official registration number as a licensed nonprofit (fillable field for later).

Historical and recent documentary photos.

6. Fund/Foundation Page

Organizational structure and board of trustees (photos, names, titles).

Bylaws and regulations (downloadable PDF links).

Strategic plan.

Committees (social, financial, media, etc.).

7. Family Members / Family Tree Page

An interactive or semi-interactive family tree display (branches and generations).

A searchable directory of family members (optional, with data privacy considered).

Ability to add short bios for elders and founders.

8. Achievers Page

A grid display of family members with notable achievements (photo, name, achievement, field).

Categorized by field (medical, academic, sports, business, etc.).

9. Initiatives and Projects

Cards for each initiative (title, description, image, status: upcoming/ongoing/completed).

A detail page for each initiative.

10. News

Two sections: Family News and Fund/Foundation News.

Each article: main image, title, date, full content, and social sharing.

Category, archive, and pagination system.

An admin panel to add, edit, and delete news easily without needing a developer.

11. Library

Upload and display documents (PDF), photos (gallery), and videos (embedded from YouTube).

Categorized by type and year.

12. Contact and Contribution

A contact form with captcha to prevent spam.

Phone number, email, and a Google Maps link to the headquarters (if applicable).

A "Contribute" page for collecting donations or contributions, with electronic payment options (optional for later, depending on local regulations).

Links to the family's WhatsApp/Telegram communication groups.

13. Technical Requirements

Architecture: A modern web application (React/Next.js or equivalent) with server side rendering (SSR) for better SEO and load speed.

CMS: A simple Arabic language content management system that lets non developers update news, pages, initiatives, and the library.

Database: To store news, family members, initiatives, and media.

Hosting: Deployable on cloud hosting (such as Vercel or a Saudi/Gulf hosting provider that supports .sa domains).

Domain: Ready to run on a custom domain (e.g. alboabid.family or alboabid.sa).

Security: SSL certificate (HTTPS), spam protection, password protected admin panel access.

Performance and SEO: Search engine optimization (Arabic meta titles and descriptions), compressed images, fast load times, full mobile compatibility.

Accessibility: Good color contrast, scalable font sizes, screen reader compatibility.

Backups: Automatic periodic backups of content and database.

Analytics: Integration with Google Analytics or a similar tool to track visitors.

Social Media Integration: Links and icons for the family's social accounts (Twitter/X, Instagram, Snapchat, TikTok, YouTube, WhatsApp).

14. Additional Notes

Maintain a formal and modest tone in images, colors, and text.

Leave all text and data (names, numbers, founding date, official registration number) as editable placeholders until the actual Al Boabid family data is provided.

Build the site to be extensible in the future (e.g. adding an online store, family member login, electronic voting for the board of trustees, etc.).
